1Quick start

  1. Open the app. It starts with a built-in sample dataset so every screen shows real examples straight away. The yellow chip in the page header tells you when you are viewing sample data.
  2. Download the template. On Data Upload & Quality, download the sample CSV or the blank template to see the expected columns.
  3. Upload your stock file. CSV or Excel (.xlsx), one row per batch/lot. Column names are matched flexibly (e.g. Lot No, Batch and Batch_No all work).
  4. Review the data-quality report. Fix any red errors at source (e.g. missing expiry dates). Warnings are handled with safe defaults but should be corrected.
  5. Set your policy. In the sidebar under Thresholds & settings, adjust slow-moving, overstock and safety-stock days to your pharmacy's policy, and pick your currency.
  6. Act. Start on the Executive Dashboard alerts, then work through each module. Every table has a CSV download; the sidebar offers a complete multi-sheet Excel action report.

2Preparing your data

Each row is one batch of one product at one location. A product with three batches on the shelf has three rows. Demand (Units_Sold_90D) may be split across batch rows or put on one row — PharmaStock sums it per product.

Expired stock is never "usable"
Expired units cannot be dispensed, so they are excluded from usable quantity, days on hand and availability. A product whose only batch has expired shows as stocked out — exactly how a patient would experience it.

3The six modules

⏳ Expiry & Compliance

Buckets every batch: Expired, ≤30, 31–60, 61–90, 91–180, >180 days, Unknown.

Projected unsold = batch qty − max(0, ADU × days to expiry − qty of earlier-expiring batches)

The FEFO projection assumes you dispense First-Expiry-First-Out at the current rate and shows which batches will expire on the shelf before it happens.

🐢 Slow-Moving

  • Non-moving: no sales in 90 days, or last sale older than the non-moving threshold.
  • Slow-moving: days on hand above the slow-moving threshold (default 180).
  • Fast-moving: annual turnover ≥ 12×.

Act before stock turns into expiry loss: transfer, return or stop reordering.

📦 Overstock

Target max = Max_Stock_Level, else ADU × overstock days (default 120)
Excess value = (usable qty − target max) × unit cost

Highlights cash tied up in specialty biologics, cold-chain items and seasonal lines (e.g. flu vaccine after the season peak).

🚨 Stockout Risk & Reorder

Safety stock = ADU × safety days (V 14 · E 7 · N 3)
Reorder point = ADU × lead time + safety stock
Order qty = ADU × (lead time + cover days) + safety − usable
  • Stocked out: no usable stock (and demand exists, or the item is Vital).
  • Critical: days of cover shorter than supplier lead time — you will run out before a normal order arrives.
  • Reorder now: at or below reorder point.

Priority = urgency × VEN weight. P1 is same-day action (emergency order, borrow, or therapeutic substitution).

🧮 ABC-VEN

ABC ranks items by annual consumption value (ADU × 365 × unit cost): A = top ~80% of spend, B = next ~15%, C = remaining ~5%. VEN adds clinical criticality. See the matrix below.

🔄 Turnover, DOH & Availability

Turnover = annual consumption value ÷ usable stock value
DOH = usable qty ÷ ADU
Availability = SKUs with usable stock ÷ all SKUs

Healthy retail pharmacies typically turn stock 8–12× a year (30–45 DOH); targets for Vital availability are ≥ 98–100%.

5Compliance playbook

⛔ Expired stock on hand
Remove from dispensing stock immediately and place in a clearly labelled, segregated quarantine area. Record batch, quantity and reason. Return to supplier for credit where the agreement allows, otherwise arrange destruction through a licensed pharmaceutical waste contractor and keep the destruction certificate.
🔒 Expired controlled drugs (narcotics / psychotropics)
Keep in the controlled-drug cabinet, segregated and marked "EXPIRED – NOT FOR USE", until destruction. Destruction normally needs an authorised witness and an entry in the controlled-drug register (drug, strength, batch, quantity, date, signatures). Balances must continue to reconcile until the stock is destroyed.
❄️ Cold-chain products (2–8 °C / frozen)
Before returning, transferring or dispensing near-expiry refrigerated stock, check the fridge temperature log for excursions. Excursion-affected stock must be quarantined pending manufacturer advice. Vaccines and biologics carry the highest value per unit — overstock here is the most expensive kind.
⏳ Near expiry (≤ 90 days)
Enforce FEFO (First-Expiry-First-Out) on the shelf; mark short-dated packs; stop reordering the product until the batch is consumed; negotiate returns before the supplier's return window closes (often 3–6 months before expiry); consider inter-branch transfer to a site with higher demand.
🏷️ Batch traceability & recalls
Every received batch must be recorded with its batch/lot number and expiry. Rows flagged "No batch no." cannot be traced in a manufacturer or regulator recall — correct them at goods receipt.
📁 Record keeping
Keep a dated copy of the Excel action report (sidebar) as evidence of routine expiry checks, stock reconciliation and corrective action. Many inspection frameworks expect documented, periodic date-checking of all stock.

This playbook describes widely used good pharmacy and good distribution practice. Always follow your national regulator, controlled-drug legislation and your organisation's SOPs where they differ.

6Suggested daily routine (10 minutes)

  1. Dashboard alerts: clear every red banner first — Vital stockouts and expired stock.
  2. Stockout Risk: place P1 orders today; queue P2 orders; confirm lead times with suppliers for critical items.
  3. Expiry: pull expired batches to quarantine; action the ≤30-day list (dispense first, return, transfer).
  4. Weekly: review Overstock and Slow-Moving to stop over-ordering and free cash.
  5. Monthly: review the ABC-VEN matrix and turnover by category with the pharmacy & therapeutics committee.

8Troubleshooting

  • "Required column(s) not found" — rename your headers to match the template, or use one of the accepted aliases (e.g. Qty, Lot, Expiry, Cost, Sold 90D).
  • Dates look wrong — ambiguous dates like 03/04/2027 are resolved by whichever order (day-first or month-first) parses more of the column. Use ISO dates (YYYY-MM-DD) to remove ambiguity.
  • Everything is "Essential" — your file has no VEN column. Add V/E/N classifications agreed with your clinical team.
  • Old Excel .xls files — save as .xlsx or CSV first.
  • Analysis dated in the past or future — change the reference date in the sidebar to analyse a stock snapshot as of a specific day.
